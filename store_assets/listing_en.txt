FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
One tap. Two orbs. Frustratingly hard.

HOLO is a one-tap reflex and rhythm game. You control two orbs bound by a tether: every tap swaps them and reverses their spin. That's the whole control scheme. Simple to learn, hard to master: threading them through tight corridors, sweeping lasers, walls that roam on two axes and hazards that chase you takes real timing.

★ 100 LEVELS THAT RAMP UP STEP BY STEP
It starts gentle and keeps pushing. Every level tests the same tap in a new way: shrink and grow zones, shields, energy barriers, boost pads, breakable walls. Go for 3 stars on each one.

★ ENDLESS MODE
A new map every run. The higher you climb, the faster it gets. Earn diamonds every 100 metres, beat your own record line and fight for the top of the weekly leaderboard. The weekly top 10 win prizes.

★ OLO IS WATCHING
Olo, a cheeky little alien, rides along. He teases you when you're stuck, is stunned when you set a record and drops a hint when you really struggle. You can switch him off in the settings.

★ GHOST RACES AND WORLD RANKINGS
Every score is verified by replaying it on the server - no cheating. See where you stand worldwide, in your country and in endless mode, and race the ghost of the world's number one.

★ ORB COLLECTION
Dozens of orb skins: level rewards, achievements, legendary pieces, and a new deal of the day every day.

★ DAILY MISSIONS AND LOGIN REWARDS
New daily and weekly missions plus a login calendar.

★ YOUR PROGRESS IS SAFE
Link your Google account and your progress follows you to a new phone.

★ COCKPIT FEEL
A dark neon interface, music for every sector and haptic feedback. In English, Turkish, German and Spanish.

Free to play. The game contains ads; watching a video for diamonds is always optional. Remove-ads, orb skins and diamond packs are available as in-app purchases.

If you like short but hard games, skill and reflex games, and levels that make you say "one more try", HOLO is for you.

Two orbs. One pulse. Which level will stop you?

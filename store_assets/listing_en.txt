Two orbs. One tether. Endless space.

In HOLO you control two orbs bound by a single tether. Every tap swaps
their positions and reverses their spin — that's the entire control
scheme. Deceptively simple, endlessly demanding: across 100 levels the
challenge escalates from careful timing to full-on rhythm mastery.

★ ONE TAP, INFINITE DEPTH
The whole game is one tap. But every level tests it differently: tight
corridors, sweeping lasers, moving walls, breakable barriers, and more.

★ POWER-UPS THAT ACTUALLY MATTER
Shrink and growth zones, shields, boost pads — each one is a real key
to clearing specific levels, not a throwaway bonus. Learn to use the
right power at the right moment.

★ 100 LEVELS ACROSS 5 SECTORS
A journey from near orbit to the deepest, most dangerous corners of
space. Every sector has its own look and its own soundtrack.

★ CHASE STARS AND HIGH SCORES
Aim for 3 stars on every level, beat your best score, and freely
replay any level you've already cleared to push for a better run —
your progress is never locked away.

★ GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
Your scores compete automatically against players worldwide. Check
your live rank and climb toward the best pilots.

★ A COSMIC ORB COLLECTION
Personalize your style with 80+ orb skins — from common looks and
level rewards to achievement unlocks and legendary collectibles.

★ A FULL AUDIOVISUAL SPACE TRIP
A neon-outlined, dark glass interface; procedural music unique to
each sector; haptic feedback for a real cockpit feel.

Free to play, with optional orb and diamond purchases. No ads — just
a clean experience built entirely around your reflexes and focus.
